From **May 12 to 16, 2026**, Palermo and the Madonie mountains will provide the backdrop for the historic Sicilian race, founded by Vincenzo Florio in 1906. This edition will stand out not only for its sporting significance, but also for its cultural and scientific value, thanks to the collaboration between UniPa and ACI Sport.

This represents a prestigious opportunity for the team, which will be able to showcase its vision in the field of e-mobility, offering visitors the chance to discover the project and its developed solutions up close.

> "The Targa Florio is the event that best embodies the spirit of Sicily in the world of motorsport. Having a dedicated space for our team is an important opportunity to promote our mission: bringing innovation to this sector through the commitment and talent of the best Sicilian students." 
> 
> **Gabriele La Milia**, Logistics Division Manager.
